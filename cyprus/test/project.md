# test

Open `index.html` over HTTP. One test per module, at the module's own path; each runs in a worker of its own.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `index.html`, `index.js` | the test page (Alpine): every test, OK or what failed |
| `uses.js` | for a test by use cases: a module's functions, each counted when called; which none called |
| `worker.js` | the worker one test runs in |
| `core/geometry.js` | `core/geometry.js` by use cases, as the house uses it: the gable wall cut by the roof; a wall less its window; the facade's EPS split between the floors; a wall's face in its plane (polygons); a room's floor (rectangles); the compass, a stable random number: every call in one |
| `core/surfaces.js` | `core/surfaces.js` by use cases, as the scene uses it: a block drawn (faces, welded corners); two blocks of one wall (the joint gone), of two (a contact piece each, raw); a chimney on a slab (no T-junctions); a roof's faces by their side; the welding; a face's normal: every call in one |
| `core/hierarchy.js` | `core/hierarchy.js` by use cases: the house built from the root down (the model, a room, its cabinets, the library's sink); where a thing is (cabinets by each wall, quarter turns); the panel (layer, floor); Finish (a setting from above); Documents (every element, its refs); the test (what to correct, what to know): every call in one |
| `core/tools.js` | `core/tools.js` by use cases: a number in a document; a file of a format read and checked (else it says where); a carcass drawn from the wall taken into the model; a worktop's section, a pipe, a lofted bowl; a product's shape with its door, its light, its envelope, its anchor; twice, as it was: every call in one |
| `library/catalog.js` | `library/catalog.js` by use cases: the library's files read as they lie (every call, unknown keys, frozen, a texture read once, what a product names exists, each file against its type); a broken library; an estimate, Finish, a tile layout, the 2D plan's fills: every call in one |
| `library/shapes.js` | `library/shapes.js`: every product with a drawing, its parts sound; an oven, a microwave, a basin tap, a shower screen, a WC bowl, a pipe; then the API by use cases, as a room asks for it (a product checked, a door hung, a drawer of a system, runners, a pull-out shelf, a sink with its tap and trap, a basin, boards and tiles, a fixture): every exported function in one |
| `settings.js` | `index.json`, the app's settings: its layers (the blocks, each layer's form, every key once, the three floors; a hierarchy with a layer or a floor the file has not); its view (edges in their layer, the cameras of the house and its floors, the plans) |
| `house/assembly.js` | `house/assembly.js` by use cases, as index.js uses it: the site and its house assembled and sound; the panel by layer and floor; the house in its axes against `archon/analysis.md`; what is drawn of it; the rooms and their m²; what the house tells a room of itself (its ceiling, its slopes, its openings): every call in one |
| `room/cabinets.js` | `room/cabinets.js` by use cases, on the kitchen's file: read and laid out; the tree the scene hangs (a carcass under the worktop or the plasterboard over it); the model's axes; the boards the maker cuts; the appliances in their places; what goes inside (the hardware as the library's products, their holes); the same file, the same nodes; the designer's mistakes (what stops the build, what is skipped) |
| `room/assembly.js` | `room/assembly.js` by use cases, as the house assembles its rooms: the kitchen's scene places its groups; the groups meet as drawn; the hood to the ceiling; the other rooms' scenes; the lamps; Finish's metal; the designer's tiles checked; the floor's and the walls' tiles laid (under the roof's slope, the corners meeting, the tile on the face toward the room) and counted: every call in one |
| `room/tiles.js` | `room/tiles.js` by use cases: a floor of whole tiles, laid from a point inside it, rows shifted, along y, a small cut, an L, the floor into the doors, a bathroom's walls round an L and a wall's face less its door: every exported function in one |
| `view3d.js` | `view3d.js` by use cases, without a canvas: two blocks drawn (welded, or a contact piece each); a turned node; the house shown; the panel switching layers, floors, the Attic, the facade, edges; the menu's cameras; the site; a product in the room's metal; the page without a canvas, closed: every call in one |
| `core/sheet.js` | `core/sheet.js` by use cases: an estimate's sums as a price changes; a formula reading another sheet; a cycle named; numbers and links as the page shows them; a sheet as the XLSX's worksheet: every function in one |
| `docs.js` | `docs.js` by use cases: a room's book (Elements: its hierarchy with its sizes; Test: the checks, what to correct under each); the screen and the XLSX need a page |
| `house/docs.js` | `house/docs.js` by use cases: the rooms' areas against archon's, the tiles laid with their prices and sums, the inner doors as bought (or to choose), the notes: every function in one |

An API's test reads as its use cases: what a caller does, in the order it does it, each step checked; at its end, that every exported function is in one. A test's default export gets `check(what, ok)`. What the module imports and exports is left to ES modules and `tsc`.
