# room

What stands in the rooms. A room's things know nothing of the house; its scene says where they stand.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `<room>.cabinets.json` | the designer's cabinets for the maker (format `cabinets`, version 1): `materials` (a part's kind → a board of the library), `settings` (how they are built, the designer's choices: `hardware` and `appliances`, the products chosen, `installation`, `hardwareLayout`; `carcass.depth`, `carcass.offset`; what a product's maker says (a drawer's box, a hinge's drilling, a runner's lengths, a sink's cut-out) is the product's, in the library, never here), `groups` (columns; a group's own `carcass`), `components` (stacked in their columns), `notes` (the designer's) |
| `<room>.docs.json` | what a furnished room's Documents say beyond the model and the library (format `docs`, version 1; § Format docs) |
| `docs.js` | `roomSheets(room, catalog)`: a furnished room's sheets from its files (its node's `files` has `docs`); `docsOf({ cabinets, file, docs, catalog, room })`: the Estimate (`core/estimate.js`), the Cut list (a block a board: the parts by name and size, their finished edges along each size, m², banding, cuts, cut-outs, a label each), the Assembly (a card a carcass: its parts, their holes, its fronts, drawers, hardware; then the parts of no carcass, a group each), the Notes; `cutList`, `cutKey`: the same parts in one row, where to drill each |
| `<room>.scene.json` | where the room's things stand (format `scene`, version 1): `cabinets` (a group → `front`, the way its fronts face, and `offset`, its zero in the room's axes), `fixtures` (an id → `product`, `front`, `offset`, `layer`; `depth`, `length`, `height`: as it is built here), `builtIns` (`name`, `box`, `cuts`, `material`: what the owner builds before the tiles, on the floor, against the walls: its faces tiled with the walls' tiles, a niche's too; none under it, none behind it), `lamps` (an id → `kind` downlight or pendant, `at` [x, y]; a pendant's `bottom`), `metal` (the room's one metal), `tiles` (`floor`, `walls`: each its `product`, `size` (one of its formats; none: its own), `depth`, `dir` x or y, `pattern`, `offset` (the point it is laid from in every direction: the floor [x, y], the walls [x, y, h]) and `grout`, its colour). A room with no scene is empty: no tiles. The tiles end at the middle of the door |
| `cabinets.js` | the engine: `cabinetsOf(file, catalog)` → `{ groups, layout, problems, pieces, fronts, counts }`: the layout (columns, the stack, carcasses, their kinds, an appliance's `fit`, a group's `worktop`), the tree in the model's axes; the pieces to cut (§ The pieces), the fronts, the counts; every product a node placed by its anchor, drawn by the library, its maker's rules asked of the library (no product's fields here); a fault in the materials, settings, products or groups throws, one in a component is a problem |
| `tiles.js` | `layTiles(rects, tile, laying)`: the tiles of a floor or a wall's face from the scene's point in every direction (along x or y, the pattern's shift), cut by the floor; the pieces (whole, cut, small) and the count (m², tiles, whole, cut, small); `doorHalves`: the half of a door's opening on the room's side (the tiles end at the middle of the door); `wallsOf`, `rectsMinus`: the walls round a floor, rectangles less holes; `under`: a piece on the side of lines (a sloped ceiling); `tiledSurfaces(room, floor, walls)`: the surfaces to tile, each the plane of the tiles' face (the floor less what is built on it; the walls less their openings and what is built against them; where two meet one runs on over the other's end: inner corners the wall along x, outer the one along y; a casing's faces, top, a niche's reveals, floor and top); `pointOf`: a point of a surface |
| `assembly.js` | `assembleRooms(hierarchy, catalog)`: each room with a scene (its node's `files`): its cabinets built and placed under it, its fixtures and built-ins, the scene's `metal` its setting, its `tiles` checked (`tilesFaults`) and kept on the room's node, the floor's and the walls' laid under it (`floorTiles`, `wallTiles`: on each surface a slab, a solid as the house's are (from what it lies on out to the tiles' face), on its face the grout and each tile's face (form `faces`), its photo over the whole tile, the small cuts marked; their count); a hood's chimney to the ceiling; the cabinets' problems by room |

## Axes

- **The design's** (the files, as the maker measures): per group, zero at the wall (an island: the back of its worktop), at its left end as one sees the fronts, on the floor; x from the left, y from the wall toward the fronts, h up. Left-handed.
- **The model's** (the nodes): the same, y the other way (`core/tools.js`): the cabinets at negative y. A node's zero at its back, left, bottom; its size to its opposite corner.
- **In the room:** the scene's `offset` is the group's zero, always the wall line, never a carcass's back; `front` gives its quarter turn: S 0, E 1, N 2, W 3.

## The tree

A group → what runs along it (worktop, end panels, wall panel, plasterboard, strips, plinth, front panels, an island's back panel) → the carcasses under the worktop, else the plasterboard over their columns, else the group, measured from it (`carcass.offset`) → their modules (cabinet, drawer, oven, microwave) and front panels. A dishwasher's bay in the row. A space at a carcass's end: an inset panel in it.

Each node carries its bodies (`parts`, boxes in its axes): a carcass its boards (layers `boards`, `backs`, `shelves`), a module its front (`doorsAndDrawers`; a shaker's frame round a recessed panel), the rest its own board. Their material: `board:` and the board's key; the scene gives it the board's look. A group knows no other: where it meets one, its spaces stand in for it.

## The pieces

Every board the cabinets are made of is a piece to cut, made where its body is (`cabinets.js`: `Piece`): its kind (`id`: side, bottomBoard, crossRail … front, strip, worktop), its name as the maker reads it, its use and board, its sizes `a`, `b` (the thickness left out; from its box: the two larger sizes in the order x, y, h), its box in its group's design axes, its carcass (a front: its module's; a front panel, a strip: none, they are screwed to what is beside them) and its place (`G1[4]`). Its finished edges (`edges`: `side`, `length`, `along` a or b): where the room sees it (a carcass's front edges, a rail on edge its bottom, a spacer its top too, a board that slides out its top, an open drawer its fronts, a front panel where a front beside it opens, a worktop over an end panel, an island's back), less what its maker finishes (`factoryEdges`); a lacquered board instead has its edges lacquered there (`lacquer`: how many along its long and its short sides; a front all round, a strip its front). Its holes: each fitting's (the library's `holesOf`), in the piece it lands in, given from the edges its `holeFrom` names (an upright: from its front and its bottom; a front: from its hinges' edge; a lying piece: from its left and its back; a bumper's: along the edge). A worktop's cut-outs: its sink's and hob's (their makers'), a basin's holes, from its left and its back. Two holes in one place, a hole where a board lying flat meets the side: a problem.

## Format docs

`page` (`title`, `heading`, `info`, `excluded`), `params` (`waste`, `sheet`, `reserve`: a number or a formula), `values` (the room's own numbers: a sheet's size, allowances), `labels` (`fronts`: a front's function by its type and tags; `cutTitle`, `cuttingNote`, `sheet`, `legend`), `blocks` (the cut list's: `id` a block of pieces (fronts, strips, plinths, frontPanels, wallPanels, plaster, worktops, endPanels) or a use (every other piece of its board), `code` of its labels, `title`, `columns`, `cut`: its cuts counted), `categories` (the estimate's: `name`, `appliances`, `items`: `id`, `product` or `material` (a use: its board), `name`, `spec`, `use`, `qty` a number or a formula, `refs` the notes), `formulas` (named expressions), `notes` (sections of notes: `id`, `topic`, `description`, `assumed`, `decide`, `appliance` (its documents), `links`).

A text's placeholders: `{m:use}` the board's name and thickness, `{t:use}` its thickness, `{d:use}` its depth; `{s:path}` a setting of the cabinets file; `{a:role.path}` an appliance (its product, how it is built in), `{h:role.path}` a piece of hardware (its product): the makers' data; `{n:path}` a count (`cabinets.js`: counts; `tiles.<where>.<count>`), `{nodes:kind}` the fittings placed of a kind; `{g:group.path}` a group's layout, `{w:group.path}` its worktop; `{k:carcass.field}` a carcass (width, height, innerWidth, h0, h1); `{i:place/what.field}` an item by its carcass or place and its type or tag (`K25/cabinet#2`: the second from the bottom): its sizes, its front's, the space round it, the drawers behind its door, its hinges' side, a panel's edges, its appliance's `fit`; `{v:path}` the file's values, `{p:item}` a price, `{q:item}` a quantity, `{f:name}` a formula, `{note:id}` a note's number; `{block.total}` a block's m², edge, cut, qty, length; `{=expression}` with `<placeholder>`s; `|%` a percentage. A quantity `=…` is written to the XLSX as a formula: the other items' quantities, the parameters and the cut list's totals as their cells.
