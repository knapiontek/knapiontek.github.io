The Lorenz System

Three coupled equations from a simplified model of atmospheric convection, and the first well-known strange attractor.

\begin{cases} \dfrac{dx}{dt} = \sigma(y-x) \\[8pt] \dfrac{dy}{dt} = x(\rho-z) - y \\[8pt] \dfrac{dz}{dt} = xy - \beta z \end{cases} \qquad \sigma = 10,\quad \rho = 28,\quad \beta = \tfrac83

Simulation

Fixed points by Newton's method

Setting all three derivatives to zero gives the origin and, for \rho > 1, a symmetric pair:

C_\pm = \left(\pm\sqrt{\beta(\rho-1)},\ \pm\sqrt{\beta(\rho-1)},\ \rho-1\right) \;=\;(\pm 8.485,\ \pm 8.485,\ 27)

The same points can be found numerically. Write the right-hand side as \mathbf F(\mathbf u) and iterate J\,\Delta\mathbf u = -\mathbf F, where J is the Jacobian matrix:

\begin{bmatrix} -\sigma & \sigma & 0 \\ \rho - z_n & -1 & -x_n \\ y_n & x_n & -\beta \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} \Delta x_n \\ \Delta y_n \\ \Delta z_n \end{bmatrix} = - \begin{bmatrix} \sigma(y_n-x_n) \\ x_n(\rho-z_n) - y_n \\ x_ny_n - \beta z_n \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \mathbf u_{n+1} = \mathbf u_n + \Delta\mathbf u_n
