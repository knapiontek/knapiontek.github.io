Number Line: the dot shows your start; turn on the jump to see the arrow that gets you to the answer.
Squares: s² means side × side (the area). √ is the opposite — it finds the side from the area.
Order of Ops: brackets and powers/roots first, then × and ÷, then + and −.
Multiply/Divide: type each digit as you work it out, including the carry (multiply) or remainder (divide) — there's a ×divisor table to help with division.
Factors: tap a prime to test it — if it divides evenly the ladder steps down; if not, just try another. No penalty for testing, only for stopping too soon!
Fractions: +/− make you match denominators first (multiply each fraction by the other's bottom number over itself); ÷ makes you flip and multiply. The final answer just needs to be an equivalent fraction — simplified or not.
